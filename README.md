# 📊 E-commerce Sales & Customer Insights Analysis



This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to uncover revenue trends, customer behavior, and profitability insights. The objective is to simulate a real-world Business Analyst role by translating data into actionable business recommendations.



 Business Objectives

 Evaluate overall sales and profit performanceIdentify high-value customers and buying patterns
 Analyze regional and category-wise profitability
 Detect factors contributing to revenue loss



Tools & Technologies

 SQL (Data extraction & analysis)
 Excel (Data cleaning & preprocessing)
Power BI (Dashboard & visualization)





 E-commerce sales dataset containing orders, customers, products, and regional details
 Includes fields such as Sales, Profit, Discount, Category, and Customer Information




Sales trend analysis (monthly and regional)
 Profitability analysis by category and region
 Customer segmentation based on purchase behavior
 Discount impact on profit margins

KPI creation (Revenue, Profit, Customer Contribution)





 Top 20% of customers contribute approximately 40% of total revenue
Certain regions consistently generate losses due to high discount rates
 Technology category drives high sales but shows fluctuating profitability
 Higher discount levels negatively impact overall profit margins



Business Recommendations

* Optimize discount strategies in loss-making regions
* Focus marketing efforts on high-value customers
* Improve pricing strategy for low-profit categories
* Monitor KPIs regularly using dashboards for better decision-making
