# E-commerce Sales & Customer Insights Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify revenue trends, customer behavior, and profitability.

## Tools Used
- SQL
- Excel
- Power BI

## Key Insights
- Top customers contribute ~40% of total revenue
- Some regions show losses due to high discounts
- Profit varies across categories

## Recommendations
- Optimize discount strategy
- Focus on high-value customers
- Improve pricing in low-profit regions
